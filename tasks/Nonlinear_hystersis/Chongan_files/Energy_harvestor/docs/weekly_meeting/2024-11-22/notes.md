# 2024-11-22 Weekly Meeting

Source: `Old_sources/Energy harvesting 20241122.pptx`

Slides: [slides.pptx](slides.pptx)

## Summary

- Introduced the piezo-energy harvesting setup: a cantilever beam under base excitation with two piezoelectric patch pairs.
- One piezo pair is used for harvesting through a shunted nonlinear circuit; the other receives a prescribed voltage input for modulation.
- Derived the governing equations, single-mode ROM, normalized ROM, complex-amplitude averaging, steady-state equations, and stability criterion.
- Numerical examples showed hysteresis and branch selection under different initial conditions.
- The main claim was that voltage modulation near the base excitation frequency can shift the response to a higher-energy branch and improve harvested energy.

## Follow-Up

- Validate whether the ROM branch transition survives in a continuous beam model.
- Quantify the energy and power flow rather than relying only on response amplitude.
